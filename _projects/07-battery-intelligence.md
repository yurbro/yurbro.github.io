## Advanced Battery Intelligence and Technology Development

Since September 2026, I have worked as a **Research Fellow at the University of Surrey**, working with [Dr Kai Yang](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/kai-yang) at the **Advanced Technology Institute (ATI)** on an EPSRC IAA battery intelligence project.

### Project context

The project brings together electrochemical experimentation, sensing and characterisation, research databases, computational algorithms and data-driven modelling to support battery diagnostics and predictive analysis.

Its broader scope includes research software, AI-powered agents and applications for data analysis, workflow automation and decision-making, alongside experimental validation and integration of data and computational tools.

### My contribution

My work focuses on **data processing, algorithm development and research software workflows**:

- Processing and organising experimental datasets for computational analysis.
- Contributing to computational algorithms and data-driven research workflows.
- Developing research software to support analysis and workflow automation.

This ongoing work extends my data-driven engineering research to battery intelligence and connects computational methods with experimental research needs.
