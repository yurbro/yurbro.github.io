**SkinMiner: LLM-powered data mining to accelerate formulation design for skin products**

Yu Zhang, Xilu Wang, Dimitrios Tsaoulidis and Tao Chen.

SkinMiner is an LLM-powered framework for extracting, verifying and structuring dermal formulation evidence from the scientific literature, with traceable links to its sources.

**Status:** SSRN preprint; under review at _International Journal of Pharmaceutics_.
