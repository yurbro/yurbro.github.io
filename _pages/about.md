I am a PhD researcher in **Chemical and Process Engineering** at the [University of Surrey](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/), supervised by [Prof. Tao Chen](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/tao-chen) and [Assoc. Prof. Dimitrios Tsaoulidis](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/dimitrios-tsaoulidis).

My research develops **AI-driven modelling, optimisation and digital-twin methods** for engineering and healthcare. I focus on data-efficient learning, interpretable models and scientific evidence extraction, with applications in dermal drug formulation and process systems.

Alongside my PhD, I work on [clinical machine learning for IVF](/projects/clinical-machine-learning/) with [Dr Xilu Wang](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/xilu-wang) at Surrey and [Dr Mingkun Tan](https://www.medizin.uni-muenster.de/reproductionms/reprotrackms/sperm-dysfunction-and-ciliopathies/next-generation-automated-sperm-analysis/mingkun-tan-phd.html) at the University of Münster, Germany. I also work on [battery intelligence](/projects/battery-intelligence/) with [Dr Kai Yang](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/kai-yang) at Surrey.

## Research interests

Machine learning · Digital twins · Bayesian optimisation · Active learning · Symbolic regression · Scientific LLMs · AI for engineering

## Visiting research

During a **Visiting PhD Researcher** placement at [Jagiellonian University Medical College](https://en.uj.edu.pl/) (15 June–14 August 2026), I worked with [Prof. Barbara Wiśniowska](https://portalwiedzy.cm-uj.krakow.pl/info/author/UJCM2060f0f314494b8d874f90774879cfba) and [Dr Laura Krumpholz](https://portalwiedzy.cm-uj.krakow.pl/info/author/UJCM7ee164e3845b4644bcb8cd0505a2cd76/) on [SkinPhysio-Extract](/projects/skinphysio-extract/), an evidence-grounded workflow for extracting and verifying quantitative skin physiology data from literature.

## Academic background

Previously, I earned an **MEng in Control Science and Engineering** from China University of Petroleum–Beijing and a **BEng in Automation** from Panzhihua University. I also completed a research placement at West China Hospital, Sichuan University (2025).

My research and academic service include an [oral presentation at CHISA 2026 and peer-review contributions](/activities/). For a fuller record of positions, teaching, awards and scholarly service, see my [academic CV](/cv/).
