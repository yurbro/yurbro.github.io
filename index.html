Yu Zhang

PhD Researcher & Research Fellow · University of Surrey

Guildford, United Kingdom

School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering

I am a PhD researcher in Chemical and Process Engineering at the University of Surrey, supervised by Prof. Tao Chen and Assoc. Prof. Dimitrios Tsaoulidis.

My research develops AI-driven modelling, optimisation and digital-twin methods for engineering and healthcare. I focus on data-efficient learning, interpretable models and scientific evidence extraction, with applications in dermal drug formulation and process systems.

Alongside my PhD, I work on clinical machine learning for IVF with Dr Xilu Wang at Surrey and Dr Mingkun Tan at the University of Münster, Germany. I also work on battery intelligence with Dr Kai Yang at Surrey.

Research interests

Machine learning · Digital twins · Bayesian optimisation · Active learning · Symbolic regression · Scientific LLMs · AI for engineering

Visiting research

During a Visiting PhD Researcher placement at Jagiellonian University Medical College (15 June–14 August 2026), I worked with Prof. Barbara Wiśniowska and Dr Laura Krumpholz on SkinPhysio-Extract, an evidence-grounded workflow for extracting and verifying quantitative skin physiology data from literature.

Academic background

Previously, I earned an MEng in Control Science and Engineering from China University of Petroleum–Beijing and a BEng in Automation from Panzhihua University. I also completed a research placement at West China Hospital, Sichuan University (2025).

My research and academic service include an oral presentation at CHISA 2026 and peer-review contributions. For a fuller record of positions, teaching, awards and scholarly service, see my academic CV.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss research, exchange ideas, or simply say hello :)
